About Me

This is where image meets story — a space built around my obsession with audiovisual art in all its forms. I work at the crossroads of technology, narrative, and visual communication, always looking for that moment when something on a screen genuinely moves someone.

I'm a video editor, VJ, and documentary filmmaker based in Barcelona. I trained in Image Design at the Universidad Nacional de La Plata, in Argentina. Over the past five years I've worked across music videos, live visual performances, interactive installations, and documentary projects — each one a different way of asking the same question: how do images make people feel?

On the editing side, I focus on rhythm and emotion — cutting not just to what looks good, but to what lands. As a VJ, I bring visuals into dialogue with live music, turning a performance into something you experience as much as you hear. My installations invite audiences to step inside the work and interact with it directly. And through documentary, I try to give honest screen time to the stories and voices that don't always get it.

If any of that connects with what you're working on, I'd love to hear from you.

Capabilities

I like doing things. Lots of things.

Video Editor

Five years cutting across paid social, branded content, e-learning, and documentary. My main tools are Premiere Pro, After Effects, CapCut, and DaVinci Resolve — and I'm as focused on pacing and emotional rhythm as I am on technical delivery.

Motion Graphics

I design motion with intention — title sequences, brand animations, compositing, and VFX integration. I work primarily in After Effects and build things that move because they need to, not just because they can.

3D Artist

Modeling, lighting, animation, and rendering in Blender. I use 3D as a storytelling tool — product visuals, abstract environments, and character work that would be impossible or impractical to shoot.

AI-Assisted Production

I integrate AI into my production workflow — using it for image generation, asset creation, and visual ideation to move faster without losing quality. It's a tool like any other: useful when it solves a real problem.

Documentary

I make documentaries about people and the things that shape them. My approach is observational and close — I'm interested in the ordinary moments that end up saying the most.

Interactive Installations

I design experiences that put the audience inside the work. Using TouchDesigner, I build real-time, sensor-driven pieces where the boundary between viewer and artwork disappears.

caseStudy:
  kind: concept
  brief: >-
    EIGHT is a brand I invented to show how I run a campaign: a small streetwear label with three accessories — a cap,
    a fanny pack and a pair of socks — in cherry red and cream. The brief I set myself: launch it with a photo campaign
    that feels shot on 35 mm by a real team, with two recurring models, lived-in interiors and the logo legible on
    every product.
  challenge: >-
    Image models drift. A face changes from one shot to the next, a wordmark gets misspelled, a sock pattern turns into
    something else. A campaign only holds together if the same people, the same products and the same logo show up in
    every frame.
  idea: Run it like a real shoot. Cast first, make the product, build the sets, and only then take the photos — every frame generated from those references, never from scratch.
  process:
    - title: Identity
      text: Wordmark in a chunky 70s soft serif, an 8-in-a-circle monogram and a woven label. Two colours only. The logo feeds every packshot, so the letterforms match everywhere.
    - title: Product
      text: Catalogue packshots of the cap, the fanny pack and the socks on the same warm grey seamless.
    - title: Casting
      text: Character sheets for Inti and Lía — front, back, face and the details that make them recognisable (rings, tattoo, cherry earring, red nails).
    - title: Sets
      text: Ten locations with one look — a 70s living room, kitchen, bedroom and pink bathroom, a sunny side street, a Barcelona rooftop, a neighbourhood bar, a black leather sofa, a metro photo booth and a phone booth. 35 mm, direct flash, hard shadows.
    - title: Photos
      text: Day and night frames in 4:5 and 9:16. Each one takes a model, a product and a set as references; the prompt only directs pose, framing and light.
  deliverables:
    - Brand identity sheet — wordmark, monogram and woven label
    - 3 product packshots
    - 2 character sheets
    - 10 set plates
    - 9 campaign photos for feed (4:5) and stories (9:16)
    - A reusable node board in Magnific Spaces — edit one description and the chain regenerates
  credits:

      name: Lautaro Ferrandi
    - role: Image model
      name: Google Nano Banana 2.1 (via Magnific)

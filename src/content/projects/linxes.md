"Cicatrices" is a music video directed by Lautaro Ferrandi in collaboration with the band Linxes. It delves into the intricate relationship between colors, figures, and characters, resulting in an exploration of audio-visual aesthetics.

The essence of "Cicatrices" lies in its focused experimentation with projections and light, aimed at highlighting the connection between colors and figures. The video presents a stripped-down, minimalist setting that serves as the backdrop for an immersive visual experience.

The band's performance is merged with projection techniques: the interplay of vibrant hues and carefully choreographed movements evokes introspection and emotional resonance, elevating the impact of Linxes' music.

In "Cicatrices," viewers are invited to contemplate the interdependence of human experiences, both joyful and painful, and the enduring marks they leave behind.

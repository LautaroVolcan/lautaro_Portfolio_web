A generative system that merges real-time image synthesis with artificial intelligence, built on TouchDesigner and integrated with Stable Diffusion. The pipeline processes live signals and translates them into latent images that update continuously, creating an organic and unpredictable visual stream where every frame is unique and unrepeatable.

Unlike pre-recorded video or loops, this system thinks as it generates: the diffusion model's parameters are dynamically modulated from within TouchDesigner, allowing the image to mutate, drift, and respond in real time. The result is a visual space that exists on the boundary between control and chance, between authorial intent and the emergent logic of the model.

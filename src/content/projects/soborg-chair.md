The Søborg wood chair is a classic example of Danish design that has been in production since the 1950s: a minimalist chair made entirely of wood, with clean lines and elegant curves. This project studies its design using 3D software and builds a render of the chair.

It starts with research on the design of the chair and its history, analyzing its construction in detail to understand the parts that make it up. From there, a 3D model reproduces every part with its measurements, as a reference for the nuances of the design.

The next phase looks at the craft of woodwork through software — carving, sanding and finishing — to understand the skill behind a chair like the Søborg, and then builds a virtual prototype by measuring, cutting and shaping the virtual wood.

The project ends with a finished version of the chair that embodies the spirit of Danish design.

Analog meets pixels

3D renders of classic Kodak slides, each holding a photograph I shot — analog and digital in one object.

Category
3D / NFT collection
Year
2023
Tools
3D modeling, Photography

In this ongoing project, I seek to explore the intersection of analog and digital media in the emerging world of NFTs. Through the creation of a series of NFTs, each featuring a 3D render of a classic slide, I aim to pay homage to the rich history of analog photography while pushing the boundaries of digital art.

Each slide, modeled after the classic Kodak slides used in old-school projectors, serves as a portal into a world of vibrant colors, intricate textures, and thought-provoking imagery. Within each slide, I have carefully placed a photograph that I have shot, allowing the physical and digital worlds to collide in a truly unique and immersive way.

This project is a work in progress, with each new piece representing a step forward in my exploration of the intersection between analog and digital media — and a conversation about the role of technology in art.
